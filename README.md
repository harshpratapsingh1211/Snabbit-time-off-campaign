# Snabbit — The Great Indian Time-Off

## Overview

A consumer-insight-led campaign concept exploring how Snabbit can turn time saved from household chores into a meaningful consumer experience.

## Consumer Problem

Household chores consume time that consumers could otherwise spend on activities they value.

The opportunity was to shift the communication from simply highlighting a completed chore to highlighting the **time returned to the consumer**.

## Strategic Idea

### “What did you do with your time off?”

Instead of focusing only on the service outcome, the campaign makes the consumer's recovered time the hero.

The service ends, but the value continues through what the consumer chooses to do with that time.

## Campaign Journey

**Book → Time Back → Share → Proposed Reward → Amplify**

1. **Book** — Consumer books a Snabbit service.
2. **Time Back** — The chore is completed and time is returned to the consumer.
3. **Share** — Consumer shares what they did with their time off.
4. **Proposed Reward** — Selected stories could be rewarded as part of the campaign concept.
5. **Amplify** — Consumer stories can become social proof and campaign content.

## Key Insight

The opportunity is not only to communicate **“we did your chore.”**

It is to communicate:

> **“We gave you your time back.”**

This reframes the service benefit from task completion to the value of the time recovered.

## Strategic Value

The proposed campaign can create a participation loop:

**Service experience → Consumer story → Social proof → Content → Further engagement**

This makes the consumer an active participant in the communication rather than only a recipient of the service.
